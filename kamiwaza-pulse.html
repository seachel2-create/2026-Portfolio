● Product concept

Kamiwaza Pulse

A real-time employee sentiment system that tells you why morale is moving — not just that it is. Built to make the weakest part of TinyPulse its best feature.

The pitch: pulse surveys people actually answer, themes synthesized by AI, and a kudos wall that hands your CEO their All-Hands shout-outs.
Why it beats the incumbent

TinyPulse tells you the score. Pulse tells you the story.

Legacy pulse tools collect ratings and hand you a dashboard of numbers. The gap is always the same: leaders see sentiment drop but not the reason, and by the time it surfaces it's already attrition. Pulse closes that loop.

How it works

Three things it does that the others don't.

◐

Themes, not just scores

Every open response is clustered into named themes — "workload," "clarity of priorities," "recognition" — with a sentiment driver and a direction. You read five themes instead of 200 comments.

↗

Early warning, per team

Pulse watches slope, not snapshots. When a team's sentiment trends down two cycles in a row, it flags the manager and the CoS with the likely driver — while it's still fixable.

✦

Recognition that surfaces up

Peer kudos roll into a live wall. Each week the top moments are packaged for the CEO to read aloud at All-Hands — closing the loop between what teams feel and what leadership celebrates.

The loop

From a 20-second pulse to a leadership decision.

1

Pulse

Two questions, 20 seconds, delivered in the tools people already use — timed to the weekly rhythm, not bolted on.

2

Synthesize

AI clusters open text into themes with sentiment drivers, protecting anonymity by reporting at the theme level.

3

Signal

Managers get targeted nudges; the CoS gets an early-warning view of teams trending down before it becomes attrition.

4

Celebrate

Peer kudos surface up into a CEO shout-out packet for All-Hands — recognition that actually reaches the top.

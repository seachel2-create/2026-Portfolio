About

The best systems are built on trust.

Beyond the operating systems, my real strength is people. I've managed and grown teams and stepped into the hard performance conversations most leaders avoid. I've elevated consulting firms and served as an independent advisor, and I've built and deepened the partner relationships that turn into real revenue.

My path here wasn't a straight line — and that's part of what I bring. I taught adaptive skiing to people of every ability, where I learned real patience and how to meet someone exactly where they are. I hustled through New York fashion — think The Devil Wears Prada: grace under pressure, impossible deadlines met anyway. And I've thrived in tech, where all of it converges — patient and driven in equal measure, calm and human even when the pace is relentless.

What I'm looking for next is a true partnership — a leader who embraces my strategic mind, treats me as a genuine thought partner in their success, and is excited to 10x their organization in a way that's collaborative, energizing, and (why not?) actually fun.

For your organization

Here's how we could work together.

Chief of Staff is a wonderfully shape-shifting role — I can be your full operating partner or jump into just one lane and make it shine. Here's what that could look like, depending on who I'd get to partner with.

With a Founder / CEO

Your operating system & force-multiplier

I run the company's nervous system so you stay on the highest-leverage work.

  • Own the operating cadence — Top 1/5, Big Rocks, a weekly execution pulse.
  • Surface risks a week or two early and force the decisions that stall.
  • Re-architect ops around AI so we scale without adding headcount.
  • Be your thought partner in the room and the follow-through after it.
With a President

Strategy into execution, across every function

I translate your priorities into a rhythm the whole org actually moves to.

  • Turn the plan into owned programs with clear DRIs and dates.
  • Keep functions aligned and cross-team dependencies unblocked.
  • Represent your priorities downward, and hold accountability upward.
  • Carry the special projects that don't fit neatly on one team.
With a COO

Extend your operational reach

I give you a live view of execution — and the systems to act on it.

  • Stand up dashboards for project health, pipeline, hiring, and slippage.
  • Run vendor, finance & HRIS stand-ups to keep the back office investor-ready.
  • Build repeatable playbooks from one-off wins (like our SLED motion).
  • Own the ops work you'd otherwise clone yourself to cover.
With a CHRO / Head of People

Culture, talent & the hard people work

I build the people systems — and bring the emotional intelligence to make them land.

  • Own sourcing and a structured, measurable hiring pipeline.
  • Stand up recognition & pulse (like the Kamiwaza Pulse platform I built) to keep culture visible.
  • Design performance-management and comp/leveling frameworks.
  • Read the org and step into the hard conversations leaders avoid.
